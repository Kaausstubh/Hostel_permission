🔍 Bottleneck Discovery & Recommendations
1. Free-Tier Cold Starts: Render's free tier spins down on inactivity (~30s cold wake-up). For production go-live, upgrade to a Starter tier instance ($7/month) to ensure 24/7 responsiveness.

2. Redis Connectivity: Connect a cloud Redis cluster to activate BullMQ background workers and cross-container distributed locks for multi-instance scaling.

3. Read-Through Caching: Keep high-traffic student status checks cached to protect the MongoDB connection pool during sudden gate openings.
